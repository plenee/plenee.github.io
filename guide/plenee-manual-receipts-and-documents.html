Plenee reads what you send it. A receipt is matched to its card charge and itemized. A bill, statement, ID or policy is kept in Documents with its key facts.

You don't need to say which is which. Plenee works it out from the file.

How a receipt is matched

Plenee reads the store, date, items, tax, tip, total and the card's last 4 digits. It puts each item in a category. Then it looks for the card charge:

  • the amount within $5 or 2%, whichever is more;
  • the date within 3 days;
  • a store name that looks alike.

The card's last 4 digits pick the account. If no account has them, or two do, Copilot asks you to tap the right one. To stop it asking, add the number under Card numbers. The chapter on connecting your money shows how.

No charge yet

If the bank hasn't sent the charge yet, Plenee adds the receipt as a Scanned transaction, marked SCN. When the charge arrives within a few days, the two merge. If the charge is up to 30% more than the receipt, the difference is recorded as a tip.

Save it, or not

A receipt you send shows as a card in the chat, with its items and totals. Nothing is saved until you choose. A snapped photo offers:

  • Save with attachment: keeps the photo with the receipt;
  • Save just transaction: keeps the items and drops the photo;
  • Update categories: change where items are filed first;
  • Cancel.

For a file, the choices are Save it, Update categories and Discard.

Keep copies

Settings, then Keep copies, decides what happens to files you send, forward or drop in a folder. Off, the default, means Plenee reads the file, saves the itemized receipt, and deletes its copy. On means it keeps a PDF with the purchase. Snapped photos always ask.

A saved receipt

On a computer, open a transaction's receipt to see its items grouped by category.

  • Tap a category to move all its items, or tap one item to move just that one.
  • Switch between Receipt and Photo when a photo is kept.
  • Add photos or files attaches more.
  • Delete file removes the photo but keeps the receipt and its items.
  • Combine with another receipt joins two receipts of one purchase. Separate undoes it.

Forward by email

Open the menu, then Email docs. Plenee gives you a private address. Copy it, then forward receipts and invoices to it. You can also set your email to forward them automatically.

  • Mail is accepted from your login's email, and up to 5 more addresses you add. Each needs a

6-digit code sent to it.

  • You can give each address an attribution, for example a family member's name.
  • Up to 20 emails a day and 200 a month, 5 files each, 15 MB a file.

Forwarded files arrive in your Captures chat, headed "From your email:" and the subject. Check each one, then save it.

An email with no attachment but a receipt in its text becomes "Email receipt.pdf".

An email with only a link to a receipt can't be read yet. Open the link, save the receipt as a PDF or a screenshot, then forward or snap that.

Get a new address stops the old one at once.

Watch a folder

On a computer, in Chrome or Edge, Plenee can watch a folder, such as your Downloads.

  1. Open Settings, then Watch folder, then Select.
  2. Pick the folder, and allow the browser to read it.

Plenee checks once a minute, and whenever you come back to the window. New PDFs and pictures appear in a box at the bottom left. Tap Send to Captures, or Not these. Files already in the folder are left alone.

The browser remembers the folder, not Plenee, so set it up on each computer.

Share to Plenee (Android)

With Plenee on your Home Screen, it appears in other apps' Share menus. Share up to 5 photos or PDFs, then tap Send in Plenee. A share waits for an hour. Only the account owner can share.

Documents

The chips at the top filter the list. All and Receipts are always there. IDs, Contracts, Bills & invoices, Statements, Offers, Taxes and Other appear once they hold something.

Under Receipts, the ones not yet matched to a charge are listed first.

Open a document to see:

  • Details: title, kind, name, who issued it, its date, and when it's due or expires. Tap a

detail to correct it.

  • Summary, and View Key Facts: the numbers and dates Plenee read.
  • The file: Open file shows it.
  • Type, to move it to another group, and Move to a Durable.

A document's due or expiry date shows in the list, and turns to "Past due or expired" after it.

You can keep up to 200 documents. Delete document removes the file and everything read from it.

Bills

When Plenee reads a bill with an amount and a due date, it offers Track as a bill. You can choose Save as a receipt or Save as a document instead.

A tracked bill appears among your Messages until you mark it Paid. If it isn't on autopay, Plenee can remind you before it's due. See the chapter on notifications.

Statements

A card or bank statement PDF updates its account instead of going into Documents. Plenee shows the ending balance, and for a card the minimum payment, due date and credit limit. Check them, pick the account, then tap Save.

Identifiers

Open the menu, then Identifiers. This is one list of the numbers that identify you and your things:

  • driver's license, passport, Social Security number;
  • vehicle registration and title;
  • insurance policy and member ID;
  • account numbers and tax IDs.

Numbers read from your documents are added by themselves. You can add your own with Add an identifier, and sort them into groups such as Personal, Work, House and Vehicles.

Each number shows only its last 4 digits, and a date of birth is hidden completely. Tap Show to see it.

Only the account owner can see this list. Viewers never can.

Durables

A Durable is something you bought that lasts, like an appliance or a laptop. Plenee finds them in your receipts.

DeviceWhere
ComputerDurables in the top bar
PhoneMore, then Durables

Each list row shows how long the warranty runs. Open one to see:

  • Receipts and charges: everything you paid for it. One Durable can have several charges.

Switch off Counts toward the price for a charge that shouldn't count, or Unlink it.

  • Files: photos, the manual, anything you add.
  • Key facts: model, serial number and production date.
  • Check for a manual and warranty: Plenee looks up the maker's manual, support page and

registration page.

  • Warranties: the maker's warranty, a card's extended warranty or a protection plan, and when

each ends. Add a warranty adds one by hand.

  • Register this device: the details a maker's form asks for, each with Copy.

To add a charge with no receipt, open the transaction and tap Add to a Durable.

Valuables

Jewelry, watches, coins and bullion, stones, art, sculpture, furniture and collectibles can be Valuables. They count toward your net worth.

  • From a receipt: on the item, tap This is a Valuable.
  • By hand: Durables, the Valuables chip, then Add a Valuable. Or Position,

then Add asset, then Valuables.

Plenee counts a Valuable at the first of these it has:

  1. your own estimate of what it would sell for;
  2. for gold and silver, its metal value at today's price;
  3. the purchase price less 30%.

Without any of them, it isn't counted until you add an estimate.

Metal prices update once a day. Platinum, palladium and stones are never valued by themselves.

Viewers see your Valuables only as you choose. The chapter on your data covers that.
