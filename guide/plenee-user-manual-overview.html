Plenee gathers your accounts, receipts and documents in one place. It reads them for you and answers questions about them. Here is everything it does today, grouped by the job.

Where things are

On the phone, the bar at the bottom has six tabs: Plenee (your Dashboard), Chat, Transactions, Budget, Trips and More. More opens a sheet with Accounts, Position, User, Documents and Durables, and the two ways to send a file: Snap and File.

On a computer, the bar at the top has Plenee, Position, Accounts, Performance, Transactions, Budget, Documents and Durables. Your name, at the top right, opens the menu.

In the tables, Menu means that menu on a computer, and User on the phone. Settings means User, then Settings on the phone, and Profile in the menu on a computer.

Most features work on both. Where one doesn't, the tables below say so.
