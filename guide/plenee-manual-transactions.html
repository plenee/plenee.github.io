Finding, fixing and planning your transactions

Transactions holds every charge and deposit from every account, newest first, grouped by day. Each day shows the balance after it.

What the ledger shows

On a computer, each row has a description, tags, a type, a category, the account and the amount. Most of them do something when you click:

  • the ≡ icon opens the edit dialog;
  • the description shows only that merchant;
  • the category lets you change it;
  • the amount splits the transaction.

A short badge tells you where each row stands:

BadgeMeaning
PSTPosted by the bank
PNDPending at the bank
PRJProjected: a payment still to come, as a forecast
FIXFixed: confirmed, not a forecast
SCNScanned: from a receipt, waiting for the bank's charge

Tap a badge to show only that kind. The ledger shows the last 12 months to start.

Search and filter

On a computer

The bar above the ledger filters by account, type, status, category, tag, who it's for, and date. Search finds a description. Tick Exact to match the whole name only.

Views saves a set of filters under a name, so you can open it again.

On the phone

Tap Filter in the bar at the bottom. Search & filter opens.

  1. Type in Search by name.
  2. Choose Any to find names that contain your words, or Exact for the whole name.
  3. Pick types, categories, accounts, tags, trips or dates if you want.
  4. Tap Apply.

Once you've typed 3 letters and paused, Plenee shows "Matching:" and how many transactions match.

Search looks at the name, the bank's own text, split lines and notes.

On the Transactions screen, the bottom bar also has Back and Forward, to step between the filters you've used.

Change a transaction

DeviceHow
ComputerClick the ≡ icon on the row
PhoneTap the row

You can change the name, type, category, tags, who it's for, and notes.

This one, or all alike

Plenee can apply the same change to every transaction like this one, now and in future.

  • Computer: Replace changes this transaction only. Bulk update changes every match and

remembers it as a rule.

  • Phone: the switch at the bottom reads This transaction | All matching.

The edit dialog shows how many transactions match. You can widen the match by editing the bank text it looks for.

If the new rule overlaps an older one, Plenee says so. Replace anyway swaps the old rule for the new one.

Transfers

When money leaves one of your accounts and arrives in another, that's a transfer, not spending. Plenee pairs the two sides when the amounts match within 3 days, or 5 days for payment apps. It names them "Xfer:" and both accounts.

Card payments, loan payments and Zelle keep their own names.

To mark a transfer by hand, set the type to Transfer and pick the other account. This works with offline accounts. It can be set on one transaction at a time only.

Split a transaction

One charge can cover several things, such as groceries and a gift.

  • Computer: click the amount. Add lines, each with its own category and amount.
  • Phone: open the transaction, then tap Split transaction. Add lines with Add line.

Plenee shows what's left to split. Tap Save split when it reaches zero.

Tax, tip, shipping and fees

These are parts of a charge, not separate categories. Tap + tax, + tip, + ship, + fees or + insur on a transaction to record them.

Tags

Tags are your own labels, across categories: a project, a person, a trip.

A tag is one word of up to 16 characters. It can use letters, digits and - _ = ! @ # $ % ^ & *.

Planned and repeating transactions

You can add a payment that hasn't happened yet, or one that repeats.

DeviceWhere
ComputerThe + icon in the filter bar, or the + tab in the edit dialog
PhoneTransactions, then More, then Transaction. Or on a transaction, Create new

Fill in a name, type, account, category and amount. Then pick how often:

  • One-off, Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Annually;
  • a start date;
  • how long: Indefinitely, until an end date, or a number of times.

Two switches say how firm it is:

  • Non-optional or Optional: must it be paid?
  • Fixed or Projected: is the amount set, or an estimate?

A repeating bill can also be marked as on autopay.

A date in the past is always Fixed. To plan a Projected or Optional payment, pick a future date.

Foreign charges

Plenee marks a charge made abroad as Foreign. A foreign charge gets no estimated US tax or tip.

To change it, use the Foreign transaction switch. On a computer it's on the Extra tab of the edit dialog; on the phone it's in the transaction. If Plenee had a reason for its choice, it shows the reason and asks you to confirm.

Copilot may show a card listing recent charges it marked as foreign. Tap Domestic on any that aren't, or All correct.

Trips

Trips are on the phone only: the Trips tab, or Transactions, then More, then Trips.

Plenee groups your foreign charges into trips. A trip ends after 3 days with no foreign charge. A booking saved in Documents adds an Upcoming trip before you leave. Trips within the US aren't tracked.

When Plenee can't tell the country, Copilot asks once: "Which country was your trip on…?" Tap a country, tap Other…, or type a place.

Open a trip to see its transactions. You can:

  • Edit trip: its name, dates and countries;
  • Split trip on a date;
  • Merge with next trip;
  • Not a trip: the transactions stay foreign, and Plenee won't list those dates as a trip again;
  • Cancel trip, for an upcoming one.

Compact or Comfortable

On the phone, Transactions, then More, switches the list between Compact and Comfortable. The same choice is in Settings, under Display density.
